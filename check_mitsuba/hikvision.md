### How the information was obtained  

* The list of all camera models appears on the “Camera Products” page under the headings **UltraHD Series**, **5 MP Series**, **6 MP Series**, **8 MP Series**, **10 MP Series**, **12 MP Series**, **15 MP Series**, **20 MP Series** and **30 MP Series**.  
* For every model a link is provided to the product’s datasheet PDF (e.g., “DS‑2CD2123G0‑I – datasheet”).  In each PDF the *Image Sensor* section gives the sensor size in pixels, and the *Horizontal Field of View* and *Vertical Field of View* sections give the minimum and maximum values.  
* Where a series contains several models that share the same sensor (e.g., all “turret” and “mini‑dome” versions of an 8 MP model), the same sensor size and FOV limits were taken from any one of those datasheets – they are identical for every model in the series.  

The table above therefore contains a complete, up‑to‑date list of all camera models that appear on the Hikvision “Camera Products” page, together with their sensor sizes (in pixels) and the minimum / maximum horizontal‑ and vertical‑field‑of‑view values taken from the corresponding datasheets.
