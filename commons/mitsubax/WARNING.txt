WARNING: the order of the trasnformations are specified in THIS example:

    https://mitsuba.readthedocs.io/en/stable/src/generated/plugins_shapes.html
    Shape group (shapegroup)

    <!-- Create instance of the shape group, but rotated, scaled, and translated -->
    <shape type="instance">
        <ref id="my_shape_group"/>
        <transform name="to_world">
            <translate z="10"/>                     <!-- (3) -->
            <scale value="1.5"/>                    <!-- (2) -->
            <rotate x="1" angle="45"/>              <!-- (1) -->
        </transform>
    </shape>



WHILE, the current implementation uses THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION:

    translate, scale, rotate

    <!-- Create instance of the shape group, but rotated, scaled, and translated -->
    <shape type="instance">
        <ref id="my_shape_group"/>
        <transform name="to_world">
            <translate z="10"/>                     <!-- (1) -->
            <scale value="1.5"/>                    <!-- (2) -->
            <rotate x="1" angle="45"/>              <!-- (3) -->
        </transform>
    </shape>
